The problem · The licensing maze

Before Aida can work as a nurse in Ontario, she has nine steps to clear — in order, before things expire.

1Credential assessmentWES / ICAS / ICES
2Language testIELTS · CELBAN · OET
valid 24 mo
3CNO applicationafter assessment
4Jurisprudence examonce confirmed as applicant
5NCLEX-RNnational exam
6Transition to practicevalid 60 mo
7Evidence of practicewithin 36 mo
8Authorization to workunexpired when sent
9Police record checkexpires 6 mo after issue

The order and the expiry windows change by profession and by province. One expired document can send her back months.

What makes it an agent

Judgment, not a checklist.

Grounded sequencing

Builds the route in the order the regulator requires — assessment before application, application before exams.

Catches the collision

When a step slips, it finds what no longer lines up, with the actual dates — and says what to do.
